# Email Compliance

Legal requirements for sending emails vary by jurisdiction. This guide covers major email compliance laws and how to ensure your email practices comply.

## When to Use This

- Ensuring legal compliance for email sending
- Understanding requirements for different regions
- Setting up unsubscribe mechanisms
- Managing consent and opt-in processes
- Planning international email campaigns

## Important Disclaimer

This guide provides general information about email compliance laws. It is not legal advice. Consult with a qualified attorney for advice specific to your situation and jurisdiction.

## CAN-SPAM Act (United States)

The CAN-SPAM Act applies to commercial emails sent in the United States.

### Key Requirements

**1. Accurate header information**
- "From," "To," and "Reply-To" must be accurate
- Domain name and email address must identify the sender

**2. Non-deceptive subject lines**
- Subject line must accurately reflect email content
- Cannot use misleading subject lines to get opens

**3. Clear identification as advertisement**
- Must clearly identify email as an advertisement
- Exception: Transactional emails don't need this

**4. Physical mailing address**
- Must include valid physical postal address
- Can be your street address, P.O. Box, or private mailbox

**5. Opt-out mechanism**
- Must provide clear way to opt out
- Must honor opt-out requests within 10 business days
- Cannot charge fee or require personal information to opt out
- Cannot require user to visit more than one page

**6. Honor opt-out requests**
- Must process opt-out requests promptly
- Cannot sell or transfer email addresses after opt-out
- Only person who opted out can opt back in

### Penalties

Violations can result in fines up to $53,088 per email (as of 2025). Each separate email in violation is a separate offense.

### Best Practices

- Include physical address in every marketing email
- Make unsubscribe link prominent and easy to use
- Process unsubscribes immediately (don't wait 10 days)
- Keep records of opt-out requests
- Don't send to addresses that have opted out

## GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation - EU)

GDPR applies to emails sent to individuals in the European Union, regardless of where your business is located.

### Key Requirements

**1. Lawful basis for processing**
For marketing emails, you need:
- **Consent** (explicit opt-in) - most common for marketing
- **Legitimate interest** - can apply in limited circumstances
- **Contract** - for transactional emails related to a contract

**2. Explicit consent**
- Must be freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous
- Pre-checked boxes don't count
- Must be separate from other terms and conditions
- Must be as easy to withdraw as to give

**3. Consent requirements**
- Clear what user is consenting to
- Separate consent for different purposes
- Must be able to prove consent (records)
- User must be able to withdraw consent easily

**4. Right to access**
- Users can request what data you have about them
- Must provide data in commonly used format
- Must respond within one month

**5. Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")**
- Users can request deletion of their data
- Must honor requests unless you have legal basis to retain

**6. Data processing transparency**
- Must inform users about data processing
- Privacy policy must be clear and accessible
- Must explain purpose of data collection

**7. Unsubscribe**
- Must provide easy way to unsubscribe
- Must process immediately (not 10 days like CAN-SPAM)
- Must be free and not require login

### Penalties

Fines can be up to €20 million or 4% of annual global revenue, whichever is higher (as of 2026).

### Best Practices

- Get explicit opt-in for all marketing emails
- Use double opt-in (recommended)
- Keep records of consent (who, when, how)
- Make unsubscribe immediate and easy
- Have clear privacy policy
- Document your data processing activities

## CASL (Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation)

CASL applies to commercial emails sent to or from Canada.

### Key Requirements

**1. Consent**
Two types of consent:
- **Express consent** - explicit opt-in (preferred)
- **Implied consent** - limited circumstances (existing business relationship, inquiry)

**2. Express consent requirements**
- Must be clear and positive action
- Cannot be pre-checked
- Must clearly state what user is consenting to
- Consent expires after 2 years (unless renewed)

**3. Implied consent**
Applies when:
- Existing business relationship (purchase within 2 years)
- User made inquiry within 6 months
- User published email address (with no "no spam" notice)

**4. Identification**
- Must identify sender clearly
- Must include contact information
- Cannot use false or misleading information

**5. Unsubscribe**
- Must provide unsubscribe mechanism
- Must be functional for 60 days after email sent
- Must process within 10 business days
- Must be free and easy to use

**6. Record keeping**
- Must keep records of consent
- Must be able to prove consent if challenged
- Keep records for 3 years after consent expires

### Penalties

Fines up to $10 million CAD for businesses, $1 million CAD for individuals (as of 2026).

### Best Practices

- Get express consent when possible
- Keep detailed consent records
- Process unsubscribes within 10 business days
- Include clear sender identification
- Track consent expiration dates

## Other Regional Requirements

### Australia (Spam Act 2003)

**Requirements:**
- Consent required (express or inferred)
- Must identify sender
- Must include unsubscribe mechanism
- Must honor unsubscribe within 5 business days

### United Kingdom (PECR + GDPR)

**Requirements:**
- GDPR applies (same as EU)
- PECR (Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations) also applies
- Similar requirements to GDPR
- Must include opt-out for marketing emails

### Brazil (LGPD)

**Requirements:**
- Similar to GDPR
- Requires legal basis for processing
- Consent must be explicit for marketing
- Right to access and deletion

## Unsubscribe Mechanisms

All major email laws require unsubscribe mechanisms. Requirements vary slightly.

### CAN-SPAM Requirements

- Must be clear and conspicuous
- Must work for at least 30 days after email sent
- Must process within 10 business days
- Cannot charge fee
- Cannot require personal information
- Cannot require visiting more than one page

### GDPR Requirements

- Must be easy to use
- Must process immediately (not 10 days)
- Must be free
- Must be as easy as giving consent

### CASL Requirements

- Must be functional for 60 days
- Must process within 10 business days
- Must be free
- Must be easy to use

### Best Practices (Universal)

**Make unsubscribe:**
- Prominent (visible in email footer)
- Easy to find
- One-click when possible
- Immediate (process right away)
- Confirmed (send confirmation)

**Don't:**
- Hide unsubscribe link
- Require login
- Charge fees
- Ask for unnecessary information
- Delay processing

## Consent Management

Proper consent management is critical for compliance, especially under GDPR and CASL.

### Recording Consent

**What to record:**
- Email address
- Date and time of consent
- Method of consent (form, checkbox, etc.)
- What user consented to (newsletter, promotions, etc.)
- IP address (helpful but not required)
- Source of consent (which form/page)

### Consent Storage

**Best practices:**
- Store in database with timestamps
- Keep audit trail of consent changes
- Link consent to user account if applicable
- Retain records as required by law (varies by jurisdiction)

### Withdrawing Consent

**Requirements:**
- Must be as easy to withdraw as to give
- Must process immediately
- Must remove from all marketing lists
- Can keep for transactional purposes if legitimate interest

## Data Retention Policies

Different laws have different requirements for data retention.

### GDPR

- Keep data only as long as necessary
- Must have retention policy
- Must delete when no longer needed
- Users can request deletion

### CASL

- Keep consent records for 3 years after expiration
- Must be able to prove consent if challenged

### Best Practices

- Have clear data retention policy
- Delete data when no longer needed
- Honor deletion requests promptly
- Document retention periods
- Regularly review and clean up data

## Privacy Considerations

### Privacy Policies

**Must include:**
- What data you collect
- How you use data
- Who you share data with
- User rights (access, deletion, etc.)
- How to contact you about privacy

**Best practices:**
- Make privacy policy easily accessible
- Use clear, plain language
- Update when practices change
- Link from email signup forms

### Data Sharing

**Be transparent about:**
- Email service providers you use
- Analytics tools
- Any third-party services
- Data processing locations

**Best practices:**
- Only share with trusted providers
- Use providers with good privacy practices
- Include in privacy policy
- Consider data processing agreements (GDPR)

## Compliance Checklist

### For All Marketing Emails

- [ ] User has opted in (explicit consent where required)
- [ ] Unsubscribe link is prominent and functional
- [ ] Sender is clearly identified
- [ ] Physical address included (CAN-SPAM)
- [ ] Privacy policy is accessible
- [ ] Consent is recorded and documented
- [ ] Unsubscribe requests are processed promptly

### For GDPR Compliance

- [ ] Explicit opt-in consent obtained
- [ ] Consent is documented (who, when, how)
- [ ] Privacy policy is clear and accessible
- [ ] Unsubscribe is immediate and easy
- [ ] Data processing is documented
- [ ] User rights are explained (access, deletion)

### For CASL Compliance

- [ ] Express or implied consent obtained
- [ ] Consent records are kept
- [ ] Unsubscribe is functional for 60 days
- [ ] Unsubscribe processed within 10 business days
- [ ] Sender is clearly identified

### For CAN-SPAM Compliance

- [ ] Accurate header information
- [ ] Non-deceptive subject lines
- [ ] Physical address included
- [ ] Unsubscribe mechanism provided
- [ ] Unsubscribe processed within 10 business days

## International Considerations

If you send emails internationally:

**Consider:**
- Which laws apply to your recipients
- Most restrictive requirements (often GDPR)
- Regional differences in requirements
- Consent requirements vary by region

**Best practice:** Follow the most restrictive requirements (usually GDPR) to ensure compliance across all regions.
