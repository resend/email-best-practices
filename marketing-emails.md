# Marketing Email Best Practices

Creating and sending marketing emails: opt-in requirements, unsubscribe mechanisms, content design, segmentation, A/B testing, and compliance.

## Core Principles

### 1. Consent First

Marketing emails require explicit opt-in consent. Never send marketing emails without clear, documented consent.

**Requirements:**
- User must explicitly opt in
- Consent must be informed (user knows what they're signing up for)
- Consent must be documented
- Easy unsubscribe must be provided

### 2. Value-Driven Content

Marketing emails should provide value to recipients. Focus on what benefits the user, not just what benefits your business.

**Best practices:**
- Provide useful information
- Share relevant updates
- Offer exclusive content or deals
- Respect user's time and attention

### 3. Respect User Preferences

Give users control over what they receive and how often.

**Best practices:**
- Allow frequency preferences
- Provide content preferences
- Make unsubscribe easy and immediate
- Honor preferences promptly

## Opt-In Requirements

### Explicit Opt-In

Marketing emails require explicit opt-in in most jurisdictions (especially GDPR, CASL).

**What counts as explicit opt-in:**
- User checks a box (not pre-checked)
- User clicks "Subscribe" button
- User sends an email requesting subscription
- User completes a form with clear subscription intent

**What doesn't count:**
- Pre-checked boxes
- Opt-out model (user must uncheck)
- Assumed consent from transaction
- Purchased or rented lists

### Informed Consent

Users must understand what they're signing up for.

**Disclose:**
- What type of emails they'll receive
- How often they'll receive emails
- Who is sending the emails
- How to unsubscribe

**Example opt-in text:**
- ✅ "Subscribe to our weekly newsletter with product updates and tips"
- ❌ "Sign up for emails" (too vague)

### Double Opt-In

Double opt-in (confirming subscription via email) is recommended and required in some regions.

**Process:**
1. User submits email address
2. Send confirmation email with verification link
3. User clicks link to confirm
4. Add to marketing list only after confirmation

**Benefits:**
- Verifies email address is valid
- Provides additional consent confirmation
- Reduces spam complaints
- Improves list quality

**When to use:**
- Highly recommended for all marketing emails
- Required in some jurisdictions (Germany, for example)
- Best practice for list hygiene

See [Email Capture](./email-capture.md) for more on double opt-in.

## Unsubscribe Requirements

### Easy and Immediate

Unsubscribe must be easy to find and must work immediately.

**Requirements:**
- Prominent unsubscribe link in every email
- One-click unsubscribe (preferred) or simple process
- Process unsubscribe within 10 business days (CAN-SPAM) or immediately (GDPR)
- No barriers (login, forms, etc.)

### Unsubscribe Options

**Best practices:**
- Provide unsubscribe link in footer
- Make link clearly labeled ("Unsubscribe" or "Manage preferences")
- Offer preference center (frequency, content types)
- Confirm unsubscribe action

**Preference center options:**
- Email frequency (daily, weekly, monthly)
- Content types (newsletters, promotions, updates)
- Complete unsubscribe

### Unsubscribe Process

**Simple process:**
1. User clicks unsubscribe link
2. Confirm unsubscribe (one-click preferred)
3. Remove from list immediately
4. Send confirmation email (optional but recommended)

**Don't:**
- Require login to unsubscribe
- Ask for reason (optional is okay)
- Delay removal
- Send marketing emails after unsubscribe

## Content and Design

### Subject Lines

Subject lines are critical for marketing emails. They determine whether emails are opened.

**Best practices:**
- Be clear and specific
- Create curiosity (but don't mislead)
- Keep it short (50 characters or less for mobile)
- Personalize when relevant
- A/B test subject lines

**Examples:**
- ✅ "Your weekly digest: 5 tips for better productivity"
- ❌ "You won't believe what happened!"
- ✅ "New feature: Custom templates now available"
- ❌ "Important update" (too vague)

### Pre-Headers

Use pre-headers to reinforce subject lines and add value.

**Best practices:**
- Complement the subject line
- Add additional context
- Include a call-to-action
- Keep under 90-100 characters

### Content Structure

**Above the fold:**
- Clear value proposition
- Primary call-to-action
- Engaging visual or headline

**Body:**
- Scannable format (short paragraphs, bullets)
- Clear hierarchy
- Multiple CTAs (top, middle, bottom)
- Visual breaks

**Footer:**
- Unsubscribe link
- Company information
- Social links
- Contact information

### Mobile-First Design

Most marketing emails are opened on mobile. Design accordingly.

**Best practices:**
- Single-column layout
- Large, tappable buttons (44x44px minimum)
- Readable text (16px minimum)
- Images that scale properly
- Test on mobile devices

### Visual Design

**Best practices:**
- Use consistent branding
- Keep design clean and uncluttered
- Use images strategically (not required)
- Ensure good contrast for readability
- Support dark mode

**Accessibility:**
- Alt text for all images
- Sufficient color contrast
- Readable font sizes
- Semantic HTML structure

## Segmentation and Personalization

### Segmentation

Send relevant content to the right people.

**Segment by:**
- User behavior (purchases, activity)
- Demographics (location, role)
- Preferences (content interests)
- Engagement level (active, inactive)
- Signup source

**Benefits:**
- Higher open and click rates
- Lower unsubscribe rates
- Better user experience
- More effective campaigns

### Personalization

Personalize emails to increase engagement.

**Personalization options:**
- Name in subject line or greeting
- Location-specific content
- Behavior-based recommendations
- Past purchase history
- Preferences

**Best practices:**
- Don't over-personalize (can feel creepy)
- Use data you have permission to use
- Make personalization relevant
- Test personalization effectiveness

## Frequency and Timing

### Frequency

Find the right balance between staying top-of-mind and avoiding annoyance.

**Considerations:**
- User preferences (if collected)
- Content type (newsletters vs. promotions)
- User engagement (adjust based on opens/clicks)
- Industry standards

**Best practices:**
- Start with lower frequency
- Increase based on engagement
- Allow users to set preferences
- Monitor unsubscribe rates

### Timing

Send emails when users are most likely to engage.

**General guidelines:**
- Weekday mornings (9-11 AM local time)
- Tuesday-Thursday often perform best
- Avoid Monday mornings and Friday afternoons
- Consider time zones for global audiences

**Best practice:** Test and analyze your specific audience. Timing varies by industry and audience.

## A/B Testing

Test different elements to improve performance.

### What to Test

**Subject lines:**
- Different wording
- Personalization vs. generic
- Length
- Emoji usage

**Content:**
- Headlines
- CTAs (text, color, placement)
- Images vs. no images
- Layout variations

**Send time:**
- Different days of week
- Different times of day
- Time zone considerations

### Testing Best Practices

- Test one variable at a time
- Use statistically significant sample sizes
- Test consistently
- Document results
- Apply learnings to future campaigns

## Compliance for Marketing Emails

Marketing emails have stricter compliance requirements than transactional emails.

### Required Elements

**Every marketing email must include:**
- Clear sender identification
- Physical mailing address (CAN-SPAM)
- Unsubscribe mechanism
- Clear indication it's a marketing email (GDPR)

### Regional Requirements

**CAN-SPAM (US):**
- Opt-out mechanism required
- Honor opt-out within 10 business days
- Include physical address
- Clear sender identification

**GDPR (EU):**
- Explicit opt-in consent required
- Clear purpose disclosure
- Easy unsubscribe
- Data processing transparency

**CASL (Canada):**
- Express or implied consent required
- Unsubscribe mechanism
- Clear sender identification
- Record keeping requirements

See [Compliance](./compliance.md) for detailed legal requirements.

## List Hygiene

Maintain a clean, engaged email list.

### Regular Cleanup

**Remove:**
- Hard bounces (immediately)
- Unsubscribes (immediately)
- Inactive subscribers (after extended period)
- Invalid email addresses

### Re-engagement Campaigns

Before removing inactive subscribers:
- Send re-engagement campaign
- Offer value or incentive
- Ask for feedback
- Provide easy unsubscribe

### List Quality Metrics

**Monitor:**
- Bounce rate (should be <2%)
- Complaint rate (should be <0.1%)
- Unsubscribe rate (varies by industry)
- Engagement rate (opens, clicks)

## Analytics and Measurement

Track key metrics to measure success and improve.

### Key Metrics

**Delivery:**
- Delivery rate
- Bounce rate
- Spam placement rate

**Engagement:**
- Open rate
- Click-through rate (CTR)
- Conversion rate
- Unsubscribe rate

**Business:**
- Revenue per email
- Customer lifetime value
- List growth rate

### Benchmarking

Industry benchmarks vary, but general guidelines:
- Open rate: 20-25% (varies by industry)
- Click rate: 2-5% (varies by industry)
- Unsubscribe rate: <0.5% (lower is better)

**Focus on your trends:** Compare to your own historical performance more than industry averages.
